Research Experience

  • Sep 2026 – Dec 2026
    Visiting Researcher, Docling team (host Peter Staar)
    IBM Research – Zurich, AI for Knowledge. Zurich, Switzerland
    • Contributor to Docling, IBM's open-source document conversion toolkit (60K+ GitHub stars), working on several research projects around it
    • Developing a VLM that links the fields of a form to their values
  • Sep 2025 – Dec 2025
    Visiting Researcher (host Pin-Yu Chen)
    IBM Research, MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab. New York, USA
    • Took AI-generated content detection from single images or text passages to whole documents. Built AIdocFactory, an open-source pipeline that parses PDFs with Docling, rewrites chosen paragraphs, figures and tables with any Hugging Face generative model, re-renders the page and records box-level labels. Used it to build a 35K-page benchmark from 2,000 arXiv papers
    • Trained AIdocDetector, a 256M-parameter VLM that classifies each page and segments the edited regions: F1 of 0.83 on seen and unseen generators and Dice of 0.85–0.88, above GPT-5.2, Gemini 3 Flash and Claude Opus 4.5 prompted as detectors
    • First-author paper in submission, journal extension in preparation; IBM filed a patent application on the method
  • Jan 2022 – present
    PhD Researcher and FPU Fellow
    BiometricsAI Lab (formerly BiDA Lab), Universidad Autónoma de Madrid
    • DeepID Challenge at ICCV 2025 (forged ID documents): designed and trained the UAM entry, TruFor fine-tuned with a SegFormer backbone and knowledge distillation. 1st of 26 teams on the private test set of 20K real ID documents, in both tracks, and 2nd overall in manipulation localization
    • Introduced the Membership Inference Test (MINT): activation-based MINT reaches up to 90% accuracy on 22M+ face images (IEEE Access 2025); Active MINT stays above 80% from MobileNet to ViT (ICCV 2025); gMINT reaches near-perfect detection in most settings (CVPR-W 2025)
    • Co-developed MINT for language models: 7 Transformer models and 2.5M sentences, AUC 0.85–0.99 (IEEE CAI 2026), and LoRA-fine-tuned LLMs, precision 0.77–0.92 across 4 models (preprint, 2026)
    • Built ai-mintest.org, a public web tool that runs these audits on image and text models (AAAI 2025 workshop demo; IEEE COMPSAC 2026)
    • Other first-author work: AttZoom, a drop-in spatial attention layer for CNNs (ICCV-W 2025); N-Sigma, a statistical framework for measuring bias in AI models (IEEE COMPSAC 2023)
  • 2026 – present
    ReTeX, Independent Research Project
    Personal project
    • Training a vision-language model that turns images of document pages into LaTeX source, treating document conversion as image-to-code generation. Paper in preparation. Project page
